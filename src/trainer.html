Matched to Pappa's Easy Way Electronic Keyboard Method, Book 1: treble clef, Ta / ti counting, single-finger chords.

Daily 12-minute routine
  1. Notes (3 min): one 20-note round on Keys. Aim for >90% accuracy, then work on speed.
  2. Rhythm (3 min): say the counts aloud (Ta, Ta-2, ti-ti, Ra) while tapping. Move on when you score above 85% twice in a row.
  3. Sight-read (5 min): 3-4 new melodies. Before playing, check the time signature, the rhythms and the highest and lowest notes. Then Count me in and don't stop for mistakes. Use Hear it afterwards to check.
  4. Book (1 min): read the counts of the next song in the book before you play it.

Level guide: Level 1 = pp. 13-25 (C to G, five-finger C position) · Level 2 = pp. 26-27 (up to high C) · Level 3 = pp. 28-31 (down to F below middle C).
